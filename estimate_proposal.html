Masters Kerala RE EXPO26 – Full Platform Rebuild & High-Scale Infrastructure Proposal

1. Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the full-stack architecture rebuild, module stabilization, and cloud infrastructure deployment for the Masters Kerala RE EXPO26 platform. The system has been transformed from legacy static interfaces into a high-concurrency Node.js Express API, Next.js 14 frontend, and PostgreSQL database architecture.

The rebuilt platform is engineered to support over 75,000+ visitor registrations, instant dual-mode QR gate check-ins, automated `.ics` calendar invites, and live real-time attendance analytics with sub-20ms database queries.

2. Core System Objectives
  • Stabilize and optimize core event management workflows to guarantee 99.9% uptime.
  • Establish clean, modular TypeScript architecture with PostgreSQL B-Tree database indexes.
  • Deliver responsive modern UI/UX for desktop, mobile scanners, and admin management.
  • Provide Dual-Mode QR Gate Scanning (ENTRY & EXIT) with complete audit trail logging.
  • Automate instant transactional emails, digital badge QR generation, and iCal invites.
  • Provision Hostinger KVM 8 High-Concurrency VPS for 75,000+ concurrent attendees.
3. Detailed Scope of Work
  • Architecture & Database Core: PostgreSQL DB indexing, Prisma ORM, JWT Authentication, and 3-Tier Role-Based Security (SUPER_ADMIN, EVENT_MANAGER, GATE_OFFICER).
  • Visitor Management & Automated iCal Invites: Registration CRUD, instant digital badge QR codes, welcome email triggers, and Google Calendar / `.ics` attachments.
  • Exhibitor Portal & Approval System: Public registration, pending approval workflow, admin stall allocation, and exhibitor staff pass management.
  • Gate Control & Dual-Mode Scanner: Gates CRUD, live web QR scanner supporting ENTRY & EXIT validation, and live on-site attendee tracking.
  • Gate Audit Logging: Complete audit trail logging for every scan event (Visitor ID, Gatekeeper ID, Gate Name, Scan Type, Status, Timestamp).
  • Sub-Events & Schedule Engine: Date, stage, and capacity management for main expo seminars and technical presentations.
  • Admin Dashboard & Real-Time Analytics: Dynamic real-time counters for active on-site attendees, gate traffic graphs, and CSV/PDF export tools.
